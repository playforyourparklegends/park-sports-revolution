# Premium Black-and-Gold Card Finish

## Goal
Restyle the existing five-card carousel to capture the reference image’s premium physical-card quality while keeping every card completely blank: no players, names, numbers, logos, or monograms.

## Changes
- Preserve the current compact card size, five-card arrangement, pitch-black page, blank space below, and centered starting position.
- Replace the current flat black face with layered near-black surface treatments: subtle tonal falloff, soft inner vignette, restrained reflective sheen, and edge highlights that suggest a coated physical card without making the face gray.
- Refine the border into a thin antique-gold frame with varied light intensity, a darker outer rim, and subtle inset highlight so it reads as dimensional metal rather than a glowing outline.
- Strengthen the centered card’s realistic depth with a controlled black drop shadow and low gold floor reflection; keep neighboring cards quieter and recessed.
- Remove the visible “L” monogram so all five faces contain no text or imagery.
- Retain pointer-tracked gloss, but tune it to behave like a soft reflection across black coating rather than a bright spotlight.
- Keep the existing horizontal continuous-depth swipe physics, vertical swipe/button crossfade, keyboard controls, accessibility behavior, and reduced-motion handling unchanged.

## Validation
- Compare the updated screen against the supplied reference at a mobile viewport.
- Verify the card interiors remain effectively black, the gold stays restrained, no text or imagery appears, and the compact geometry has not changed.
- Exercise horizontal movement and the vertical/tap crossfade to confirm the visual polish does not alter either interaction.
