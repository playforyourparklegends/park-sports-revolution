# Legends of the Park — Monument Generator

Generates the hero image that appears at the top of a park's Team Home screen once that park has a championship-winning Legend. This REPLACES the older mascot-based hero background approach — no mascot appears in this scene at all.

## Required inputs (must be supplied by the user before generating — never invent these)
- Park name (e.g. "Lorenzi Park")
- Team name as it will appear on the jersey chest text (e.g. "Legends of Lorenzi Park")
- The specific player's face/likeness reference image
- Player's gender and approximate age range
- (Optional) pose/expression notes — default is the intense screaming, fists-clenched championship pose

## Step 1: Research the real park
Before generating, research the real park's water feature, palm/tree lines, path layout, skyline/mountain backdrop, and general terrain. State findings briefly. Reuse research already on file if this park was researched previously (e.g. Lorenzi Park's twin spring-fed lakes, palm trees, rose gardens, tennis/ball fields, Vegas skyline, Spring Mountains).

## Step 2: The Monument figure — hard rules on SCALE (critical, most commonly gotten wrong)
- **The figure must be modestly sized — roughly 25-30% of the total frame height, not colossal.** This screen has to comfortably host a roster carousel of 10-15 players below/around it, so the figure cannot dominate the frame the way a mascot once did.
- Reference proportions: figure occupies only the upper portion of the frame, positioned near the horizon/mountain line, small enough that there is clearly substantial open sky and park space remaining around and below him — NOT stretching from mid-frame down to the treeline.
- Do not scale him "colossal" or "towering over the whole park" — think a distant, respectfully-sized commemorative figure on the horizon, not a kaiju-scale monument.

## Step 3: The Monument figure — other hard rules
- The figure is a **ghostly/translucent bronze-gold apparition** of one specific real player, standing distant near the park's horizon or mountain line — NOT rising out of the water, NOT placed in the middle of the lake, and NOT solid/opaque like a physical statue.
- Translucency: semi-transparent — background scenery (mountains, sky, clouds) should be subtly visible through the figure, like a ghostly presence watching over the park. Not barely-there faint — still clearly a recognizable, imposing figure — just not solid metal.
- Built from the reference photo the user provides — match their actual facial structure, hair, and build as closely as possible. Do not generic-ify the face.
- Position: standing in the far distance near the horizon/mountains behind the park — the park itself (lake, palms, lawns, paths) sits in the foreground/middle-ground, empty of the figure. The figure looms beyond/behind the park at modest scale, not on top of its water feature.
- Pose: intense, screaming/roaring expression, fists clenched at the sides — a championship-victory moment frozen mid-shout. (Default; override only if the user specifies otherwise for that generation.)
- Wardrobe: a **traditional button-front softball jersey** (never a V-neck), rendered in the same ghostly bronze-gold translucent material as the figure's skin — not a separate solid-colored texture. Chest text reads **"Legends of [Park Name]"** in place of a normal team name.
- **No sponsor logos, no Adidas/brand marks, no real team crest** anywhere on the figure or jersey. The only branding allowed in the whole image is the official Legends of the Park logo (Step 4).

## Step 4: Official logo placement
- Composite the official **Legends of the Park** logo (gold/black shield with Nevada silhouette) centered at the very top of the frame, well above the figure's head with clear separation — do not let the logo overlap or crowd the figure.
- Crop/scale the logo as needed for clean mobile-portrait legibility — it should read clearly at small size at the top of a phone screen.
- This logo is the only text/branding element in the image besides the jersey chest text.

## Step 5: Scene composition
- Vertical mobile portrait orientation.
- Aerial/elevated wide vantage over the real park, with the park's actual water feature, palms, and grounds visible and undisturbed in the foreground/middle-ground — the ghostly figure stands beyond it, near the horizon/mountains, at modest scale (see Step 2), not interacting with the water.
- Universal mood: dramatic stormy sky above, warm sunset breaking through low on the horizon behind/around the figure, cinematic contrast, golden-hour warmth on the grounds.
- **Roughly the bottom half of the frame stays visually open, calm, and uncluttered** — smooth water, quiet flat lawn, low detail, low contrast — since this space needs to comfortably host a 10-15 player roster carousel, not just a sliver of space. Hard rule, no exceptions.
- Cinematic composition wins over strict geographic accuracy — real recognizable park features are a guide, not a constraint.
- 4K hyper-realistic photographic quality, no illustration style, no artifacts.
- No other people, no extra text, no watermarks, no UI elements anywhere in the image.

## Step 6: Output
Single clean 4K portrait image. Replace any existing hero background asset for that park entirely — do not layer on top of the old one. Leave the card carousel component completely untouched (size, position, swipe behavior, crossfade) unless the user asks for carousel resizing to fit more players.
